# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students often make frequent small payments for food, travel, education, shopping, and other needs. Without a simple record of these transactions, it is difficult to understand monthly spending or stay within a planned budget.

## Scope
CampusSpend provides a command-line Java application for recording income and expenses, organizing transactions by category, viewing monthly summaries, setting budgets, and managing personal transaction records.

## Target Users
Primarily college students who want a lightweight personal expense tracker.

## High-Level Features
- User registration and login
- Income and expense management
- Category-based records
- Monthly financial summary
- Monthly budget management
- Transaction deletion
- Persistent SQLite storage
